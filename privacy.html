LaneLab privacy policy

LaneLab stores your profile, bowling scores, ball list, statistics, and preferences locally in your browser or app on your device. This release does not require an account and does not send this data to a LaneLab server.

Photos and imports

Files you choose for import or photo preview are processed by the app for that action. They are not uploaded by this release. OCR is not currently available.

Sharing and deletion

Exports happen only when you request them. Use Reset entire app to remove locally stored LaneLab data. You can also clear the app/browser storage through your device settings.

Changes

This policy will be updated if cloud sync, accounts, analytics, or other data collection is introduced.
